Open **Settings → Appearance**, or press `⌘,`. These settings are saved on this computer, not in the workspace.

## Theme

**System** follows your computer's light or dark mode. **Light** and **Dark** stay put.

## Palette

| Palette  | Feel                |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| MyNote   | Warm ember neutrals |
| Graphite | Neutral greys       |
| Midnight | Cool blue slate     |

Every palette has a light and a dark version.

## Accent colour

The accent colours buttons, selections, checkboxes and links: Ember, Blue, Violet, Emerald, Rose or Amber. Or click the rainbow at the end to pick any colour.

## Colours

Fine-tune the theme you're looking at, on top of its palette: the page and sidebar backgrounds, menus and popups, text, secondary and faint text, borders and code blocks. Click a swatch to pick a colour; the changes show as you pick. A colour you've changed shows its code and a reset arrow to go back to the palette's, and **Reset all** undoes them all.

Light, Dark and Black each keep their own colours, so a dark background never ends up in the light theme. To tune another one, switch the theme above first.

## Text size

Small, Default or Large, for the text of every page. To go further, press `⌘+` or `⌘−` anywhere to zoom page text in or out (50% to 200%), and `⌘0` to go back to 100%. The zoom is kept when you restart.

## Full width pages

On by default: pages use the whole window width. Turn it off for a narrower column of text that's easier to read.

> [!gray] 🖋️
> To change one page instead, use **Style**, **Small text** and **Full width** in its `•••` menu. Those are saved in the page itself. See [[Pages and sub-pages]].
